## Third-party marks

`collie-ui/src/renderer/src/assets/providers/` contains third-party provider
and service logos. They are not Collie-owned branding and no permission to
reuse them is granted through this repository. They are present only for
nominative identification, must not imply partnership, endorsement, or current
connector availability, and remain subject to their owners' trademark and
brand-guideline restrictions. Record each file's source and applicable basis
before a distributable installer is approved.

## Clearance record required

For every cleared asset, add the following information to this document or a
linked durable record:

1. Repository path and SHA-256 of the reviewed file.
2. Creator or upstream source URL/repository and acquisition date.
3. License, assignment, or written permission, including attribution terms.
4. Reviewer, review date, and whether the asset may be used in source,
   installers, website, social media, and paid marketing.

Until that record exists, this document is an installer-release blocker. It
does not grant a license or make any representation about origin, ownership,
or third-party permissions.
